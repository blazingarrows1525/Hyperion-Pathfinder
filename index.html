01 Method

How a resume is read

Four steps, from the PDF you upload to the changes worth making. Nothing is stored after the request finishes.

  1. Step 1

    Upload and parse

    The PDF is read in memory with pypdf. Text and section structure are extracted; the file is not written to disk.

  2. Step 2

    Extract evidence and skills

    Skills, tools and quantified achievements are pulled from the text and normalised against the skill taxonomy.

  3. Step 3

    Compare with the JD and the corpus

    Resume and job description are embedded with all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (384 dimensions). The most similar resumes in the alumni corpus are retrieved for reference.

  4. Step 4

    Review components and actions

    The report splits the alignment score into components and lists missing evidence and the rewrites most likely to close it.

        08 Methodology
        
            
        Sources, method and questions
        
          
        

          
        
            
        
              
        
                
        Alumni resume corpus
        
                
        226 indexed records (224 valid, 2 degraded), embedded with all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (384 dimensions), indexed with FAISS and grouped into five archetypes by K-Means. Used for reference matching only.
        
              
        
              
        
                
        2026 placement snapshot
        
                
        Batch-level offers from the campus placement portal, snapshot of 17 September 2026. Offers are not distinct students, recruiter categories overlap, and some offers have no reported CTC.
        
              
        
              
        
                
        Scoring
        
                
        A deterministic rubric (scoring_rubric.json) scores textual alignment between resume evidence and the job description. The review lens changes the simulated reviewer, never the score.
        
              
        
              
        
                
        Curated material
        
                
        Company roles, sample job descriptions, interview rounds and archetype skill lists are curated for practice and marked as unverified wherever they appear.
        
              
        
            
        

            
        
              
        
                
                  How is this different from an ATS keyword scanner?
                  
                
                
        Keyword scanners count term overlap. Resume Checker also embeds the resume and the job description with all-MiniLM-L6-v2, retrieves similar resumes from the alumni corpus for reference, and scores the match with a fixed rubric. It is still a comparison of text: it cannot see how an interviewer will judge you.
        
              
        
              
        
                
                  Does a high score mean I will get an interview?
                  
                
                
        No. The score measures how well the evidence in your resume matches the language of the job description. Hiring decisions depend on assessments, interviews, timing and many factors this tool cannot observe.
        
              
        
              
        
                
                  Is my resume stored or shared?
                  
                
                
        The PDF is read into memory for the request, parsed and discarded; the server does not write resume files or extracted text to disk or a database. The job description is currently sent as a URL parameter, so it can appear in server access logs. Your browser stores only display preferences (theme, motion, 3D detail).
        
              
        
              
        
                
                  Which numbers are sourced and which are illustrative?
                  
                
                
        Sourced: the corpus counts and dimensions (corpus manifest) and every placement figure (2026 snapshot). Curated or illustrative: company roles, sample job descriptions, interview rounds, archetype skill lists and the demo report. Curated material is labelled where it appears.
        
              
        
              
        
                
                  Is a large language model involved?
                  
                
                
        Only if the server is configured with an LLM provider. Without one, reports come from a deterministic local engine. The Engine row in Preferences shows which one is active.
