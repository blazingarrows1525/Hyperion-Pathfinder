Grounded Methodology

How Resume Checker Works

A four-stage pipeline moving from raw resume extraction to high-precision placement pattern synthesis.

  1. 1

    Upload & Parse

    Your resume PDF is read entirely in-memory using pypdf. Text, technical entities, and project descriptions are cleanly extracted without saving any personal documents to persistent storage.

  2. 2

    Semantic Match

    The target Job Description is embedded into a dense 384-dimensional vector via all-MiniLM-L6-v2 and compared against our FAISS index of 226 verified benchmark alumni profiles.

  3. 3

    Archetype Benchmark

    The system isolates the candidate's primary Career Archetype (e.g. Data Science, SDE, or Full-Stack) and pulls the top nearest-neighbor alumni resumes that successfully landed offers.

  4. 4

    Improve & Optimize

    Our LangGraph agent analyzes achievement deltas, missing high-impact metrics, and missing technical skills, generating an actionable roadmap to elevate your placement readiness.
